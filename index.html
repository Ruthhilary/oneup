The problem

In Lagos, every trip is a gamble on the fare, the car, and the wait.

A professional commuting to Victoria Island twice a day can spend ₦420,000 a month — up to 84% of a ₦500k salary. Drivers hand over 20–30% commission and absorb ₦550–600/litre fuel, so a hard 25-day month still nets only ₦272k–381k. Both sides want a price they can predict.

Where OneUp’s margin comes from

Driver cost is ₦120 × kilometres actually ridden, so the unused slice of each plan is the margin engine — light riders are the most profitable.

Blended gross margin at a 30/50/20 plan mix ≈ 26%. After insurance, inspections, payments, support, driver bonuses and acquisition (~15–18%), target net margin ≈ 8–11%. The thin per-km margin on Pro means plan-mix and real utilisation are the two numbers to validate in a pilot.
